Custom Moderating Team Page Installation Guide:

This Replaces the standard "Moderator Team page" in the forum/elsewhere, with a custom Page, One Where in the ACP you can assign any groups you want to be displayed here and in what order they are displayed. 

1. Extract Contents of zip file to your computer. (if you haven't already done so).
2. Open up your ftp client, Log into your site.
3. open up "upload" folder from the zip, and upload the "admin" folder to your board's root on the ftp.
4. once done, Go to Your ACP, go to system->Manage Applications & Modules.
5. On the right hand side, you will see "custom moderator team page" and a button that says "install", click install, follow instructions on screen.
6. Once that is done, Applications ACP menu can be accessed thru "Other->Custom Moderator Team Page".
7. Enjoy
